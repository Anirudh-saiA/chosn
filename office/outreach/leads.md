### Registration, DPIIT recognition and what it unlocks (next step for the founder, research only)
Why: the founder says CHOSN is "neither yet" registered nor DPIIT-recognised (via Main, 2026-10-23). That closes the Bengaluru pod and National Startup Awards.
Facts from Startup India (pages last updated 2026-09-14): recognition is for entities incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm or LLP; "should not exceed 10 years" from incorporation; turnover "should not exceed Rs. 200 crore"; applied for through the National Single Window System (nsws.gov.in), "Registration as a Startup"; the Ministry "does not charge any fee" for the certificate. Benefits listed: self-certification under some labour and environmental laws, a 3-year tax holiday (only Private Limited or LLP incorporated after 1 April 2016, and a separate 80-IAC application), angel-tax exemption application, patent fast-tracking and a filing rebate.
Not verified: company incorporation steps, fees and time (the MCA site refused access, HTTP 403), and who qualifies for the angel-tax exemption. Take professional advice (a chartered accountant or company secretary) before deciding the entity.
Link: https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/startupgov/startup_recognition_page.html and https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/startup-scheme.html      Verified: yes, seen 2026-10-23.
