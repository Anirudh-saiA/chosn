## Reply rules
- Add one fact the post did not have, with a source. Opinions say "my take".
- Criticise a product, price or marketing line; never a person. No pile-ons, no quoting someone to mock them.
- Do not tag people into a thread we started. Do not ask for follows or reposts.
- If we get a fact wrong, reply with the correction and fix the fact bank.

## Three collaboration ideas (for the founder to decide; nobody has been contacted)
1. **"Price vs parts" month with a store.** One shoe a week, the store's list price in rupees next to the Nike or adidas spec, store provides the picture. Why: stores want honest price talk that explains value; we get their audience. Start by following and replying; propose only after a few weeks of real interaction.
2. **Community teardown thread.** Ask followers for photos of the inside tag and outsole of their pairs (style code, country of origin). We collect the origins by style code in the fact bank and credit contributors. Why: the Air Force 1 origin finding (some style codes list India) is something nobody else is tracking, and it needs community data.
3. **Creator spec check.** A creator posts a review; we reply with a spec and price card in our format and, if they like it, they quote it. Why: low effort for them, adds sourced facts to their video. Needs a creator who has already engaged with us.
