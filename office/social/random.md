# Random and culture prompts

Used by `scripts/social_posts.py` for the "random" slot. One prompt per line starting with `- `. Rules: a question or a marked opinion ("my take"), no factual claim without a source, about shoes, retail, design or the Indian sneaker scene. A prompt is not reused within 30 days. Add more any time; the founder can add lines too.

- Box or no box: do you keep the box of every pair, or bin it? My take: if you resell, the box is part of the price, so it should be part of the product too.
- One pair you bought for the look and wore twice. Name it. No judgement, only the price you paid.
- Laces: do you swap them on day one, or leave the factory pair? My take: the laces are the cheapest upgrade in sneakers.
- Which sneaker store in India has the best sizing help: store staff, size charts, or reviews? Tell us what worked.
- A shoe whose name promises more than the shoe does. Which one, and what does the name promise?
- Wearing your pair in the monsoon: do you do it, or protect it? What do you do after a soaked day?
- The best colourway you saw this year that never reached India. Show us the name.
- Sneaker pricing in India: should a store show the size availability next to the price before you click? My take: yes, nobody should open five tabs to learn the size is gone.
- Do you buy for the shoe or for the story around it? Pick one and tell us the last pair you bought.
- What is the first thing you look at on a pair in the shop: the toe box, the sole, or the stitching? What does it tell you?
- Your oldest pair that you still wear. How old is it, and what did it cost?
- Retro re-releases: do you want the original materials back, or modern comfort in an old shape? My take: both, and the label should say which.
- Cleaning your pair: one product that actually worked, and one you would not buy again.
- One piece of sneaker advice you were given that turned out wrong. What was it?
