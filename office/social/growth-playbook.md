# X growth playbook for CHOSN (Social team, 2 Nov 2026)

Adapted in our own words from the general ideas in a third-party "x-twitter-growth" skill (alirezarezvani/claude-skills, MIT licence), which the founder asked about. That skill was not installed: it names five Python scripts that are not in the file, it gives algorithm claims with no source, and parts of it (10 to 20 replies a day, "mutual engagement" with peer accounts, up to 7 tweets a day) would clash with X's spam rules and with our own. This page keeps only what fits the critic voice, and nothing here is automated. The founder posts and replies.

## Weekly profile audit (10 minutes, Sunday)
- Bio says what we do in one line (what it is made of, how it is built, what you pay in rupees).
- Pinned post is current; link added once the domain exists.
- Look at the last 14 days: which posts got replies, which got none. Paste impressions and replies into `weekly.md`.

## Thread format (for a teardown or price reality check; one a week at most)
1. Hook: the surprising fact with a number ("Two Air Jordan 1 Lows, two spec sheets").
2. Three to six posts, one fact each, each with its source named; opinions say "my take".
3. Close with a question the reader can answer in a line.
Every claim keeps the confidence line from the draft; nothing without a source.

## Replies (the real growth lever, done slowly)
- Up to 5 useful replies a day, only to accounts in `engage.md` whose handle the founder has confirmed.
- Each reply adds one sourced fact or a direct answer. Never "first!", never tag people into our posts, never ask for follows or reposts.
- Reply to everyone who answers our questions within a day; the community questions only work if we do.

## Cadence
- Two posts a day (about 09:00 and 19:00 IST) from the daily draft. More is not better.
- At most two hashtags, optional, at the end.

## Do not
- No engagement pods, follow-for-follow, bought engagement, or copied threads.
- No claims about how X's algorithm weights things; we have no source, so we do not plan around them.
- No link-stuffing; links cost money in the API and read as promotion. Sources go in a reply.

## What to measure (the founder pastes; I log)
Impressions, replies, profile visits and follows per post, by post kind (drops, sale, market, fact, controversy, question). After four weeks, do more of the kinds that get replies and fewer of those that do not.
