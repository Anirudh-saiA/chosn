# Who else compares sneaker prices or tracks drops in India, what do they offer and charge, and what do they lack?
**Answer / recommendation:** There are two small direct competitors, both new and both free to users: **Snatchfy** (compares 30+ Indian sneaker stores, e-mail price alerts, no price history) and **KicksWorthy** (price history for six-plus brands, but no alerts, no named stores, once-a-day refresh). The big names around the space, **Culture Circle** (marketplace with an app, drops calendar and price alerts), **Mainstreet**, **Crepdog Crew**, **Superkicks**, **VegNonVeg** and **Limited Edt**, are sellers, not neutral comparers. Nobody I found combines **price history with an honest buy-or-wait view, size-aware stock alerts, a clearly separate resale lane, and drops/news in one place**. Those are CHOSN's three gaps (below).
**Confidence:** medium. **Why:** the two direct competitors' own pages were read on 2026-10-23, but I read home and about pages only, one site (Shoe Deal Finder) returned no content, and I did not use the products.

**Labels:** Confirmed = the company's own page; Reported = a third-party article or app store listing; Unconfirmed = not verified.

## Three gaps CHOSN can fill
1. **Honest price history plus a buy-or-wait view, with real MRP.** Snatchfy shows no history; KicksWorthy has history but refreshes about once a day, names no stores and has no alerts. CHOSN already stores dated price snapshots from 12 retailers and keeps a Buy now / Wait outlook log (repo: `price_snapshots`, `outlook_log`). That is a bigger, dated record than either small tool states, and the real MRP (see `2026-10-23-india-mrp-and-specs.md`) makes "is this actually a deal" checkable. Caveat: the competitors may grow quickly, and our history is only weeks old.
2. **Size-aware, in-stock alerts with resale kept apart.** Snatchfy offers target-price e-mail alerts; none of the readable competitors showed size filters or back-in-stock alerts. CHOSN's watch feature already supports a size, a target price, and "back in stock, price drop, target reached, sold out" alerts (repo: `watch_alerts`). Marketplace prices are a median 1.35 times store prices for the same style and size (`2026-10-23-resale-vs-retail-prices.md`), so a separate, labelled resale lane is something a pure retailer comparison would get wrong.
3. **Drops, news and prices in one place.** Culture Circle has a drops calendar inside a shop; Snatchfy has a Hot Drops feed; global tools cover Western drops. None readable combines an India-specific drops calendar and editorial news (The CHOSN Times) with the price record, and none is neutral because the big drop calendars sit inside sellers.
Extra, smaller: a plain-language "real versus fake basics" guide (`2026-10-23-real-vs-fake-basics.md`): Snatchfy sends users to outside checkers, so a neutral guide plus a legit-check checklist is open ground.

## Risks and unknowns
- Both direct competitors are young and may be one-person projects; their products can change. I did not use them, test their prices or check their accuracy.
- I could not read Shoe Deal Finder, Find Your Kicks or Snatchfy's "Pass" plan, so competitor pricing may exist that I missed.
- Store count for Snatchfy conflicts across pages ("30+" for sneakers, "50+" overall).
- I did not check whether Superkicks, VegNonVeg or others run their own price-alert features.
- Market size, funding and traffic were not researched.

3. Product (founder with Data): protect gap 1 by keeping the nightly job running so the history is long before a competitor's.
