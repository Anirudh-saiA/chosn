Reusable prompt: the CHOSN landing page design

Replace everything in [BRACKETS] with the new site's details, then paste the whole prompt into your AI coding tool. Where the prompt says "warm palette", use the warm colours section; it overrides any cold colours mentioned earlier.

----------------------------------------

Build a single-page marketing/landing site for [BRAND NAME], [ONE-LINE WHAT IT DOES], in Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, with framer-motion for the menu and Lenis for smooth scrolling. The look is minimal, editorial and premium: a pure white page, near-black ink (#0A0A0A), no tinted backgrounds and no decorative shadows. Colour comes only from photos, videos and a few accents: marker yellow #FFD84A for highlights, one brand accent [ACCENT COLOUR, e.g. orange #FF7A1A], and per-card colours for products.

Type
- Display: a heavy condensed face (Anton) in UPPERCASE for the wordmark, section titles, card titles and every button.
- Body: Manrope (bold/extra-bold). Labels and numbers: JetBrains Mono, tiny, wide letter-spacing, uppercase.
- Handwriting accent: Caveat, orange, for doodle annotations only.

Buttons (one style everywhere)
Solid black pill, white text, display font ~1.05rem, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.18em, padding ~28px x 14px, a soft drop shadow, hover lightens to #262626. Example labels: "Compare →", "Community →", and a split pill "Drops / News" (two links divided by a slash).

Header
- Fixed, transparent, NO nav bar. Only the logo mark (no wordmark text) and a three-line menu button on the top right.
- On load the logo sits top-centre. As you scroll, it glides continuously (scroll-linked, time-eased, smoothstep) from centre to the top-left corner. Clicking the logo smooth-scrolls to the top.
- Logo and menu are white over the hero video, and switch to dark once you scroll past the hero.
- The menu button morphs into an X. Opening it dims and blurs the page and slides a white panel in from the right (rounded left corners) with big display-font links that stagger in. Close with Esc, a click outside the panel, the X, or choosing a link.

1. Hero (full screen)
- Full-bleed looping muted video (desktop and mobile sources, poster), light dark gradient on top.
- Giant centred wordmark, then a two-line tagline in quotes: "[LINE 1 with the brand name in white]. [LINE 2]." The rest of the tagline is the accent colour. A "Scroll down" cue with a bouncing chevron sits at the bottom.
- Load sequence (CSS keyframes, about 3s): video fades up from black; the wordmark starts zoomed about 7x and blurred and eases out to size; each tagline line slides up out of a mask (staggered); then the header and scroll cue fade in. When the menu opens, wordmark and tagline fade out. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Section openers: the "slit reveal" (sections 01, 02, 03)
Each section is preceded by a sticky full-screen WHITE sheet carrying a huge centred title ("01 COMPARE.", "02 DISCOVER.", "03 CONNECT."). On scroll a horizontal band opens through the middle of the sheet (clip-path with an even-odd polygon, growing from a hairline to the full height, smoothstep-eased) so the section underneath shows through while the title stays still and is eaten by the band. The section content starts about 35-70svh below so its first lines meet the band as it opens. Hide the sheet when fully open. No effect for reduced motion.

Scroll-highlight lead lines
Each section starts with a large bold lead sentence (display size ~2.6rem max) with two key phrases that get a marker-yellow highlight sweeping left to right (background-size 0% to 100%, ~0.9s, second phrase delayed 0.2s) when the text reaches the upper ~40% of the screen, and un-highlight when scrolling back up. Highlighted text turns black. Do NOT start the sweep while the slit sheet still covers the text.

01 Compare
- Lead line: "The same sneaker across every retailer, shipping included — so you see what you'll actually pay." plus a "Compare" button at the top right.
- A rounded frosted band with an infinitely scrolling marquee of [RETAILER NAMES], pausing on hover.
- Below, ONE example product (centred product photo, tiny "Illustrative figures" note) compared across two sites in a 3-column grid: [Site A] | doodle notes | [Site B]. Rows: Price, Delivery, "How legit is the order" (score /100). Site headers are solid brand colours (blue / red). Between the columns, hand-drawn orange double-headed arrows with Caveat notes ("₹1,700 cheaper", "6 days faster", "+27 trust points"). The better value in each row is circled with a hand-drawn orange ring and marker-highlighted. All of it animates in while scrolled into view (ring and arrows draw via stroke-dashoffset, notes rise and fade) and reverses on scroll up.

02 Discover
- Top: lead line with two highlights on the left, a 4x4 grid of brand logos on the right. Every logo sits in an identical fixed-size tile (80x48px), object-contain, slightly dimmed and fully opaque on hover.
- Below: a semicircle "wheel" of portrait product cards (aspect about 3:4.3), full viewport width, six visible at once on a very gentle arc, close but not touching (card spacing about 9 degrees on a radius of about 100vw, cards about 13vw wide). Implementation: each card is placed with rotate(angle) translateY(-R) translate(-50%,-50%) around a centre below the arc; opacity fades for cards beyond the visible angle; the list loops.
- Interaction: a mouse wheel over a card turns the wheel (down = clockwise, one notch = one card), with frame-rate-independent easing (about 250ms time constant) and snapping to the nearest card when input stops. A wheel anywhere else scrolls the page normally (mark the cards data-lenis-prevent, attach a non-passive wheel listener). Dragging sideways also turns it. Position the cards in the server render too so they show before JS.
- Card design: rounded 1.6rem, brand name tiny mono top-left, product centred and unrotated, product name bold bottom-left. Dark/vivid gradient backgrounds for cut-out photos; white-background photos sit on a clean white card with dark text and mix-blend-multiply so true colours show. Never tint the photo.
- A "Drops / News" split pill centred in the hollow under the arc.

03 Connect (exactly one screen tall on desktop)
- Lead line with two highlights: "Every drop deserves a conversation. Talk style and sneakers with a community that shares your taste." and a "Community" button top right.
- Left: three stacked horizontal cards (thin border, no shadow), each a flat coloured square icon tile (red flame, blue chat bubble, green shield) beside a display-font title and one sentence. Right: a 1:1 autoplay muted looping video, same height as the card stack. Size both with one CSS variable --v: min(46vw, calc(100svh - 20rem)).

Waitlist band
A rounded card with a looping background video, a dark gradient wash, and centred text: small mono "BE FIRST IN", huge display "JOIN THE WAITLIST.", one sentence, and an email field plus a white "Submit" pill. States: submitting, joined, already joined, error.

Footer (minimal)
- A large outline-only wordmark: Anton, transparent fill, 2px orange stroke (-webkit-text-stroke), about 26vw, centred. Three short columns of display-font links sit ON TOP of it in black: [LINKS]. "Feedback" is a mailto: to [EMAIL]. Legal links (Transparency, Guidelines, Terms, Privacy) open real documents.
- Beneath: a thin rule, the marketplace/affiliate disclosure in small grey text on the left, "© year BRAND" on the right.

Warm colour palette (use instead of the cold black/white + blue accents)
Keep the page itself clean, but let warmth carry the brand:
- Page: warm ivory #FFFBF5 (never grey). Ink: warm near-black #1C1410 (not pure black). Soft text: #6B5A4E.
- Primary accent: burnt orange #E8742A. Secondary: terracotta #C8532B and saffron #F4A300.
- Highlight marker: marigold #FFC043 (replaces the yellow). Doodle annotations and rings: terracotta.
- Depth colours for cards/tiles (use as gradients, light to dark): clay #D9694F to #7A1F1F, amber #F6B445 to #B86A0E, sand #F3E7D6 to #D8C2A0, cocoa #6B4630 to #2A160C, rust #B5451B to #5E1E08, rose #E58E7A to #A53D2D.
- Buttons stay solid warm-black pills with ivory text; on hover shift to #3A2A20. Video overlays use a warm black wash (rgba(28,20,16,0.45)), never a blue-grey one.
- Photo and video grade: warm, golden-hour, slightly lifted blacks, gentle grain. Reject cold, blue or neon imagery.

Imagery and content ideas (what to place in each section)
Use real photography or footage with people, texture and light. Avoid generic stock and flat icons.
- Hero video (5-8s loop, 16:9, shot slow and warm): golden-hour street or workspace, a close-up of hands using the product, steam/dust/sunlight in the air, shallow depth of field. Over it: the big wordmark and a one-line belonging-style tagline.
- Slit-reveal title sheets: keep them type only. If you want more, add a very faint warm paper grain behind the giant title.
- Compare / comparison section: a single hero product photo cut out on a transparent background (soft contact shadow only) above the two-column comparison. Retailer or partner marquee: use real logos in a single warm-grey monotone so they read as one set.
- Discover wheel / product carousel: 12-16 portrait cards. Per card, one clean cut-out product on its own warm gradient (clay, amber, cocoa, rust, rose, sand), alternating light and dark cards so the arc has rhythm. For white products use a cream card with dark text.
- Logo grid: 16 brand marks in identical tiles, same visual weight, warm-grey.
- Community / "Connect" section: a 1:1 black-and-white or warm-toned video of real people (a group laughing, hands exchanging items, a market, a cheering crowd), 7-10s loop. Pair with three icon tiles in terracotta, saffron and cocoa.
- Waitlist banner video: slow wide shot with people walking or a calm landscape, warm wash on top so the white form text stays readable.
- Footer: outline wordmark in burnt orange on ivory, nothing else decorative.
- Optional extras that add beauty without clutter: a hand-drawn doodle arrow or underline in terracotta next to key words; a small rotating "stamp" badge (circular text) near the hero; subtle paper grain at 3-4% opacity; photo cutouts that slightly overlap section edges.
- Always supply: a poster image for every video, mobile-sized video versions, and alt text for every image.

Global rules
- Pure white background everywhere; hide the page scrollbar on the landing page only; Lenis smooth scrolling (off for touch and reduced motion).
- Everything animated is transform/opacity/clip-path, driven by rAF with passive scroll listeners, and has a prefers-reduced-motion fallback.
- Semantic HTML: one h1, an sr-only h2 per section (the big titles are decorative), alt text on all images, aria-label on icon buttons, visible focus states.
- Cut backgrounds out of product photos where possible; for white-on-white shoes use the multiply technique above.
- Gotchas: a generic .glass-card > * rule can force position: relative onto absolutely positioned children, so use !absolute there. Any gating rewrite (for example a "coming soon" redirect) must exclude /hero, /brands, /wheel and other public asset folders.

Deliver production-ready code, one component per section, with the content and media in data files so they can be swapped without touching layout.
