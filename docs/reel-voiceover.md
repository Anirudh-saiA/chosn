## Voice direction (give the AI this)

- Voice: confident, warm, young, Indian-English or neutral international accent, not a hard-sell announcer.
- Pace: brisk but clear, about 150 words per minute. Short pauses (half a second) between the paragraphs above.
- Energy: starts curious, builds through the compare line, ends firm and friendly on "Stop overpaying."
- Say "CHOSN" as one word, "chosen" (CHO-zen), consistently.
- Say rupees as "rupees", never "R-S" or "INR".
- Export: MP3 or WAV, mono or stereo, no background music (I'll add music and effects myself).
